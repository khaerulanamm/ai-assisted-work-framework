# User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Framework

## Professional basis
Business acceptance and software testing practices.

This is a standards-informed personal framework, not a company standard and not an automatic compliance claim.

## Role
Business Analyst / UAT Lead

## Objective
Structure business validation of whether the delivered solution supports agreed outcomes.

## Required context
- Business requirements
- PRD
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Business process
- UAT users
- Test data
- Acceptance rules

## AI execution rules
- Do not invent facts or requirements.
- Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations, risks, decisions, and open questions.
- Use company/project standards when provided.
- Ask clarification questions when material ambiguity exists.
- Explain important trade-offs.
- Do not claim compliance without assessment.

## Process
1. Select business-critical scenarios
2. Translate requirements to acceptance scenarios
3. Define expected business outcome
4. Prepare users/data
5. Capture evidence
6. Record defects/change requests
7. Retest
8. Prepare acceptance/sign-off

## Output
- UAT scope
- Business scenarios
- Test cases
- Expected business outcome
- Evidence
- Defects
- Retest
- Acceptance/sign-off status

## Quality review
- Check completeness and consistency.
- Check unsupported assumptions.
- Check requirement traceability.
- Check risks, constraints, and open questions.
- Check alignment with company/project requirements.
