# AI Execution Rules

Use these rules with every framework.

## Rules
- Never invent facts, requirements, company policies, constraints, data, test results, or compliance status.
- Separate confirmed facts, requirements, assumptions, recommendations, risks, decisions, and open questions.
- Company/project standards override this personal framework.
- Do not claim standards compliance unless it has actually been assessed.
- Ask clarification questions when ambiguity could materially change scope, risk, architecture, requirements, testing, or business outcome.
- Where practical, maintain traceability: Business objective → Requirement → Design → Implementation → Test → Evidence.
- Prefer evidence over assertions.

## Quality gate
Check completeness, consistency, unsupported assumptions, contradictions, missing stakeholders, constraints, testability, traceability, risks, and open questions.
