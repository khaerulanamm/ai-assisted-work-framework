# Company Tailoring

This repository is a reusable starting point, not an organizational standard.

Before using a framework in a company project, provide when available:
- official template
- SOP/process
- governance requirements
- terminology
- approval/sign-off flow
- mandatory fields
- security/privacy requirements
- regulatory requirements
- versioning/retention rules

## Adaptation
AI should:
1. identify company requirements;
2. map them to the framework;
3. preserve mandatory company structure;
4. identify gaps/conflicts;
5. generate the artifact in the required company format.

Do not submit an AI-generated artifact without human review.
