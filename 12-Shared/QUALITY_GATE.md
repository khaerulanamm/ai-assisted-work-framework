# Universal Quality Gate

Before delivering any AI-assisted artifact:

## Accuracy
- Facts supported?
- Calculations/data correct?
- Anything invented?

## Requirements
- Confirmed requirements covered?
- Testable?
- Contradictions identified?

## Context
- Fits the actual project?
- Uses company terminology?

## Governance
- Company template/SOP followed?
- Security/privacy/regulatory needs considered?

## Traceability
- Important requirements trace to design, implementation, tests, and evidence?

## Uncertainty
- Assumptions and open questions explicit?

## Human approval
- Reviewed by the appropriate owner/stakeholder?
